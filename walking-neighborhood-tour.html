THE EXPERIENCE

Good food. Hidden streets. Local stories.

Wander the neighborhood with a local host and enjoy a relaxed evening discovering Vietnamese favorites. It is about tasting, sharing and seeing a side of Saigon beyond the tourist trail.

What makes this experience special

Hidden alleys

Explore everyday neighborhood corners and local streets.

Vietnamese food

Get a taste of Saigon’s local favorites, from savory street food to sweet treats.

Local stories

Our local hosts bring Saigon’s food culture to life with stories, traditions, and warm conversations.

Food highlights

  • Vietnamese noodle soup and fresh spring rolls
  • Seafood selections and Vietnamese bánh mì
  • Sugarcane juice, flan and a cold beer at a popular local rooftop
YOUR EVENING, STEP BY STEP

How the adventure unfolds

A look at how your evening may unfold with our local team.

  1. Meet Your Guide

    Join your local host at the hostel lobby.

  2. Walk Through Hidden Alleys

    Explore everyday neighborhood streets and corners beyond the main tourist areas.

  3. Taste Local Favorites

    Try a selection of Vietnamese dishes and drinks from the tour’s food highlights.

  4. Share Food & Local Stories

    Your local host will share stories about Saigon’s neighborhoods and food traditions as you explore together.

  5. Chill With a Cold Beer on a Local Rooftop

    Finish the evening at a local rooftop beer spot. Take in the Saigon atmosphere, unwind with your fellow travelers, and enjoy a cold beer included in the tour. Non-alcoholic drinks are available for guests who prefer them.
